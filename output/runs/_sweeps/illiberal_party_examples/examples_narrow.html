'ERT episode' is an autocratization episode starting within 5 years of the election. Both outcome columns are LOG changes over the same window, ln(value at election year + 5) - ln(value at election year - 1); positive polyarchy means the index ROSE, i.e. no backsliding on that measure. These are single elections, shown to make the cases concrete -- they are illustrations, not evidence, and any one of them can run the other way. 'PopuList 411' marks the elections in the restricted sample where one top-2 party scores above 0.6535 and the other does not. Where the opponent's score is also near the ceiling (Morocco 1993: 0.985 vs 0.853) the sign of the running variable rests on a small difference between two parties both coded as anti-pluralist; SCORE_GAP_MIN in 12_rdd_analysis.R is the lever for excluding those.
